# Synthetic fixture contract

All names, quantities, destinations, documents, and relationships are fabricated training records. No operational customer data is included. `synthetic/dataset.ts` exports the immutable-by-convention `syntheticDataset` (v1) and a separate `repairedDataset` (v2). Consumers must not mutate them. `ground-truth/expected.json` contains expectations manually enumerated from the defined relationships rather than generated by the engine.

Source `ING-A19` feeds two production batches. Both products feed a mixed-case repack. There are twelve ingredient lots, three synthetic suppliers, three warehouses, six shipments, and multiple SKUs. The known source reaches FG-101, FG-102, FG-201 and SHIP-001–004. FG-102 is a potential association. FG-201 has a confirmed source path through FG-101 but incomplete full genealogy through the other merged branch. SHIP-001 is already shipped and must remain historical after every intervention. Quarantining WH-NORTH with EV-DRILL at 2026-10-28T10:00:00Z only prevents planned SHIP-002.

Known bad data: missing SHIP-002 destination; absent SHIP-003 lot identifier; no WH-SOUTH response owner; a potential USE-A19-102 link; a dangling ING-Z99 genealogy reference into BATCH-404; and an identical duplicate SUPPLY-ING-F02 record. FG-404 and SHIP-404 remain unresolved, even though they are disconnected from the chosen source. FG-900 and SHIP-900 share a warehouse with affected stock but must not become associated through that warehouse. Their exclusion from the source trace does not assert safety.

V2 repairs only those recorded gaps, using EV-REPAIR. No historical shipment is removed or altered. Baseline quarantine evidence remains UNKNOWN until a simulated quarantine is supplied; no applicable action is never interpreted as PASS.

## CSV import

`synthetic/sample.csv` is a complete importable file. The strict two-column header is `record_type,json`. Records use RFC4180 quoting (double embedded quotes); LF or CRLF and UTF-8 BOM are accepted. Exactly one `metadata` row supplies `version`, `name`, and `synthetic`. Remaining rows contain a `node`, `edge`, or `evidence` JSON object matching `src/contracts.ts` and `src/schemas/dataset.ts`. This explicit envelope avoids inferring missing quantities or inventing join rules from free-form ERP columns.

Limits: 200,000 UTF-8 input bytes; 1,000 nodes; 3,000 relationships; 3,000 evidence entries; 30 references per record; 500 characters per descriptive field. Structural errors, unsupported units, invalid identifiers, negative quantities, nonfinite numbers, and malformed ISO timestamps reject the import. Semantic faults remain visible as evidence gaps. Identical duplicate relationships are counted once; contradictory duplicates are excluded from definite association and their downstream records remain unresolved.

Quantity units: g/kg/lb normalize to kg; ml/l normalize to l; units remain counts. No package conversion, dose, production yield, or stock-to-shipment mass balance is inferred. Totals cover unique reachable shipment records, including potential/unknown associations, and exclude missing quantities with an explicit gap. Full recorded shipment quantity is not a claim about contaminated mass.

CSV exports prefix formula-leading fields (including whitespace/control-prefixed spreadsheet operators). Markdown escapes imported markup and does not turn documentary references into trusted executable links.

## Reproducibility

`runDrill` accepts dataset, scenario, and interventions. It reads no clock, random source, network, or filesystem. The scenario timestamp is explicit. Arrays are deterministically ordered; duplicate shipment paths do not add quantity. Dataset checksums are FNV-1a identifiers, not cryptographic signatures. Server-side authentication/idempotency must use stronger server-controlled identifiers where required. A snapshot includes the dataset plus scenario, interventions, results, initiator and capture timestamp.

## Adversarial control limits

The simulated quarantine uses a unique, evidenced `stores` edge quantity as the recorded remaining stock **at the scenario timestamp**. For example STOCK-101 records 120 kg still at WH-NORTH, independently of FG-101's 240 kg production lot quantity and the already shipped 120 kg. This is an explicit fixture assumption, not an inventory reconciliation calculation. Planned shipments are allocated against that budget in scheduled timestamp and identifier order. Earlier movements, including ones before the simulated control, consume that reservation budget. Missing quantities, incompatible dimensions, contradictory/duplicate stock links, or insufficient stock leave a whole shipment unproven as preventable. Partial shipment prevention is intentionally unsupported. Direct hold simulations require a positive known shipment quantity but do not assert physical stock availability. Every pre-scenario intervention timestamp is rejected.

Evidence conflicts are conservative: contradictory records sharing an evidence ID are not considered resolved documentary support. Full-genealogy assertions examine all recorded ancestor branches even where one confirmed exposure path is sufficient to associate the product with the source. A confirmed source association does not imply all genealogy is complete.
